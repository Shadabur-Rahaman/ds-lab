## Start with a question. Not an algorithm.

A café owner asks: **“How many drinks should we prepare tomorrow?”** Data science turns observations into a useful decision. You gather orders, explore patterns, test an explanation, and measure whether your decision helps.

### Your first investigation

The chart shows a small **synthetic café dataset**. Switch the metric to discover why a busy day is not always the most profitable day. These are teaching values, not a real business forecast.

### A useful mental model

**Question → collect → explore → explain → act.** This is a loop: results often reveal a better question. A chart is evidence; it is not automatically an explanation of cause.

> Yaad rakho: pehle sawaal, phir analysis. Decide what you want to learn before choosing a tool.

### Common mistake

A busy day can have lower revenue if people buy cheaper items. Compare both metrics before changing staffing or stock. Then check whether the pattern repeats over several weeks.
