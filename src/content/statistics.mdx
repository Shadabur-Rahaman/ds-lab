## One number can hide a whole story.

The **mean** adds every value and divides by the count. The **median** is the middle value after sorting. Both describe the center, but they react differently to unusual observations.

### Move the outlier

Five café orders cost ₹20, ₹25, ₹30, ₹35 and an adjustable amount. Move the last order from ₹40 to ₹200. Watch the mean move while the median remains ₹30.

### Choose a summary that fits the question

The median can describe a typical order when values are skewed. The mean still matters for revenue planning because it accounts for every rupee. Neither is universally better. Report the spread and sample size too.

> Mean sabko sunta hai; median beech wale ko. An outlier is something to investigate, not automatically delete.
